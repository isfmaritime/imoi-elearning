The journey in five lines

  1. FindA seafarer finds a course on our website through Google, an ad or WhatsApp.
  2. PayHe pays online on our portal by UPI, card or net banking.
  3. LearnHe studies on his phone or laptop and passes a short test.
  4. CertificateHe gets a certificate with a QR code that any company can check.
  5. Expert talkHe has a 30-minute Zoom talk with an IMOI expert.

Shipping companies do the same for their whole crew in one payment, and follow everyone's progress in one place.

Quick answers

Is the payment safe?

Yes. Razorpay takes the money, and our server checks every price before charging.

Who builds it?

ISF's own HELMS tech team, on imoi.helms.in. They have this model and a detailed guide.

What does it cost to run?

No new software fee. Razorpay's usual fee per payment, and about ₹0.12 per WhatsApp update.

Where do we change a price?

In one place only: the course's box in WordPress. The website and the portal both follow.

Are the courses DG Shipping approved?

No. They are value-added courses, and every course page says so.

When will it go live?

After your go-ahead, the build, and a full test with test payments. Google will show it only after that.

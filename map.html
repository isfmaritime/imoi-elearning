Where everything lives

Think of it as a shop. Each part has one job, and each piece of information is kept in only one place.

The shop windowOur website

imoitraining.com shows every course and its price. People find us here through Google, ads and WhatsApp.

The price tagThe E-learning box in WordPress

Our team changes a price or offer here once, and it changes everywhere.

The barcodeThe HELMS ID

A number on each course, like 2249 for PTVE SIRE 2.0. It links the website course to the right course in HELMS, so the sync never mixes them up.

The shop and the classroomThe IMOI portal

imoi.helms.in, built inside HELMS. People pay, learn, get certificates and book debriefings here.

The cash counterRazorpay

Takes the money safely by UPI, card or net banking. We never see card details.

The postmanEmail and WhatsApp

Every update reaches the learner by itself: payment, debriefing, reminders.
